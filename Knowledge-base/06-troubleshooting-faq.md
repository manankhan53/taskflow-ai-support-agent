# TaskFlow Help Center: Troubleshooting & FAQ

## General Troubleshooting Steps
Before escalating, these are the standard checks for most reported issues:
1. Refresh the page or restart the mobile app.
2. Check TaskFlow's status page at status.taskflow.com for any ongoing outage.
3. Try logging out and back in.
4. Clear browser cache, or try a different browser, if the issue is display-related.

## "The page won't load / is stuck loading"
Usually caused by a browser extension conflict or temporary connectivity issue. Try an incognito/private browser window. If the issue persists in incognito mode across multiple browsers, this is likely a genuine bug and should be escalated with details: browser name/version, operating system, and a screenshot if possible.

## "I didn't get a notification I was expecting"
Check **Profile Settings → Notifications** — notification types (task assigned, due date reminder, @mention, comment) can each be individually toggled on/off per user. If notifications are enabled there but still not arriving, check whether email notifications are being filtered to spam, or whether push notifications are enabled in your device's OS settings (TaskFlow cannot control OS-level notification permissions).

## "My changes aren't saving"
This is typically a connectivity issue — TaskFlow requires an active internet connection to save changes; it does not currently support full offline editing with sync-on-reconnect. If connectivity is confirmed stable and changes still aren't saving, this should be escalated as a potential bug, with details on exactly what action was being performed.

## "I was charged but my plan didn't update" / "I upgraded but still see old plan limits"
This is a billing/account issue — always escalate to a human. Do not attempt to diagnose or explain a resolution, since this may involve a payment processing delay or a billing system error requiring account-level investigation.

## "Can I merge two Workspaces?"
No — TaskFlow does not currently support merging two separate Workspaces into one. Data would need to be manually recreated in the target Workspace. This is a known limitation, not a bug.

## "Can I export my data?"
Yes. Under **Workspace Settings → Data Export**, Owners and Admins can export all Projects and Tasks as a CSV file. Full JSON export (including comments and attachments metadata) is available on Business plan and above.

## "Is there an offline mode?"
No, TaskFlow requires an internet connection. Changes made while briefly disconnected are not queued or auto-synced — attempting to edit while offline may result in lost changes.

## "How do I report a bug?"
Use the in-app "Report a Bug" option under the Help menu (bottom-left corner), or ask the AI assistant, which will log it for the support team. Always include: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and your browser/device.

## Questions Outside TaskFlow's Scope
If a customer asks something unrelated to TaskFlow (e.g., general productivity advice unrelated to the product, questions about a different software product, or personal questions), the assistant should politely clarify it can only help with TaskFlow-related questions and offer to connect them with a human if needed.

## When in Doubt
If a customer's question does not clearly match documented information above, or involves their specific account/data that the assistant cannot verify, the correct action is to escalate to a human rather than guess. An incorrect confident answer is worse than an honest "let me connect you with someone who can help."
