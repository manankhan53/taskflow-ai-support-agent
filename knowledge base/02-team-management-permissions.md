# TaskFlow Help Center: Team Management & Permissions

## Inviting Team Members
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**.
2. Click "Invite Member."
3. Enter their email address and select a role (see roles below).
4. Click "Send Invite."

The invited person will receive an email with a link to join your Workspace. Invites expire after 7 days. You can resend an expired invite from the Members page.

## Member Roles & Permissions
TaskFlow has four roles:

- **Owner** — Full control, including billing, Workspace deletion, and transferring ownership. Every Workspace has exactly one Owner.
- **Admin** — Can manage members, projects, and settings, but cannot access billing or delete the Workspace.
- **Member** — Can create and edit tasks/projects they have access to, comment, and upload files. Cannot manage other members or Workspace settings.
- **Guest** — Limited access to specific projects they're invited to. Cannot see other projects in the Workspace. Guests do not count toward your paid seat total (see Billing & Plans).

Only Owners and Admins can invite new members or change roles.

## Changing a Member's Role
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**.
2. Find the member and click the role dropdown next to their name.
3. Select the new role.

Note: You cannot change your own role if you are the sole Owner — ownership must first be transferred to another Admin.

## Removing a Member
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**.
2. Click the three-dot menu next to the member's name.
3. Select "Remove from Workspace."

Removed members lose access immediately. Tasks previously assigned to them remain in the system but show as "Unassigned" unless reassigned first.

## Transferring Workspace Ownership
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**.
2. Find the Admin you want to promote.
3. Click the three-dot menu and select "Transfer Ownership."
4. Confirm via the email verification sent to the current Owner.

Ownership transfer is required before the original Owner can leave the Workspace or downgrade their own role.

## Project-Level Permissions
In addition to Workspace-wide roles, individual Projects can have their own access settings:
- **Public projects** — visible to all Workspace members.
- **Private projects** — visible only to explicitly invited members, regardless of their Workspace role. Even Admins cannot see a Private project unless added to it.

Project visibility is set when creating the project and can be changed later by the project creator or a Workspace Admin/Owner, under **Project Settings → Access**.

## Guest Access
Guests are useful for inviting external collaborators (e.g., a client or contractor) to a single project without giving them visibility into your whole Workspace. To invite a Guest:
1. Open the specific Project.
2. Click "Share" → "Invite Guest."
3. Enter their email. They will only see that one project after accepting.

Guests cannot create new projects and cannot be assigned Admin or Owner roles.

## Common Permission Issues
- **"I can't see a project my teammate mentioned"** — likely the project is Private and you haven't been added. Ask the project owner or a Workspace Admin to add you.
- **"I can't invite new members"** — only Owners and Admins can invite; ask your Workspace Admin/Owner to send the invite or upgrade your role.
- **"I can't access billing settings"** — only the Owner can view or change billing information, regardless of Admin status.
