# TaskFlow Help Center: Billing & Plans

## Available Plans
- **Free** — Up to 5 members, 3 Projects, 1GB file storage total, no integrations.
- **Pro** — $9/user/month (billed monthly) or $7/user/month (billed annually). Unlimited Projects, all integrations except API access, 25MB max file size, priority email support.
- **Business** — $19/user/month (billed monthly) or $15/user/month (billed annually). Everything in Pro, plus: multiple Workspaces, API access, 100MB max file size, advanced permissions, priority chat support.
- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing. Includes SSO enforcement, dedicated account manager, custom contract terms, and uptime SLA. Enterprise plans are arranged directly with TaskFlow Sales, not self-service.

Guest accounts do not count toward paid seats on any plan.

## Changing Plans
Only the Workspace Owner can change plans. Go to **Workspace Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately; you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, not immediately. If your current usage exceeds the limits of the plan you're downgrading to (e.g., you have 8 members but are downgrading to Free's 5-member limit), you must first reduce usage below the new plan's limits before the downgrade can be scheduled.

## Payment Methods
TaskFlow accepts major credit/debit cards and, on Business plan and above, ACH bank transfer or invoiced payment (net-30 terms, arranged with Sales). Payment methods are managed under **Workspace Settings → Billing → Payment Method**.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, TaskFlow retries automatically after 3 days, then again after 7 days. If payment still fails after the second retry, the Workspace is downgraded to Free plan automatically, and any data/members exceeding Free plan limits become read-only (not deleted) until the account is upgraded again or usage is reduced.

## Refunds
TaskFlow's standard policy: refunds are not issued for partial billing periods when downgrading or canceling mid-cycle — you retain access to your paid plan until the end of the period you've already paid for. Exceptions (e.g., accidental double-charges, billing errors) are evaluated case-by-case by the billing team and are NOT something the AI assistant can approve or process — always escalate refund requests to a human.

## Canceling a Subscription
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**.
2. Confirm cancellation.

Canceling schedules a downgrade to Free plan at the end of the current billing period — it does not delete your Workspace or data. To fully delete a Workspace, see the Workspace deletion process (Owner-only, under Workspace Settings → Danger Zone).

## Invoices & Receipts
Past invoices are available under **Workspace Settings → Billing → Invoice History**, downloadable as PDF. Invoices are also emailed automatically to the Owner's email at the time of each charge.

## Annual vs Monthly Billing
Switching from monthly to annual billing can be done anytime and takes effect immediately with a prorated credit applied. Switching from annual to monthly billing takes effect only at the end of the current annual term, not immediately — annual commitments cannot be broken mid-term for a refund.

## Escalation Note
Any question involving: a specific customer's invoice/charge dispute, a requested refund, a failed-payment situation needing manual review, or a billing error — should always be escalated to a human. The AI assistant can explain policy but must never confirm a refund, credit, or billing adjustment on TaskFlow's behalf.
