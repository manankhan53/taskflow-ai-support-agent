# TaskFlow Help Center: Getting Started & Account Basics

## What is TaskFlow?
TaskFlow is a project and task management platform for teams. Teams use TaskFlow to organize work into Projects, assign Tasks to team members, set deadlines, track progress, and collaborate through comments and file attachments.

## Creating a TaskFlow Account
1. Go to app.taskflow.com/signup.
2. Enter your work email address and create a password (minimum 8 characters, must include one number).
3. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox. The verification link expires after 24 hours.
4. Once verified, you'll be prompted to create your first Workspace (see below).

If you don't receive the verification email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder. If it's not there, you can request a new verification email from the login screen by clicking "Resend verification email."

## What is a Workspace?
A Workspace is the top-level container for your team's account. All Projects, Tasks, and team members exist inside a Workspace. Most companies have one Workspace, though larger organizations with separate departments sometimes create multiple Workspaces (this requires a Business plan or higher — see the Billing & Plans document).

## Setting Up Your Workspace
When you create a Workspace, you'll be asked to:
- Name your Workspace (e.g., "Acme Marketing Team")
- Choose a Workspace URL (e.g., acme.taskflow.com)
- Select your team size (this only affects onboarding suggestions, not billing)

You can change your Workspace name and URL later in **Workspace Settings → General**.

## Logging In
Go to app.taskflow.com/login and enter your email and password. TaskFlow also supports Google Single Sign-On (SSO) — if your account was created via "Sign up with Google," you must continue logging in with Google; there is no separate TaskFlow password for these accounts.

## Resetting Your Password
1. On the login page, click "Forgot password?"
2. Enter your account email.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 1 hour).
4. Choose a new password.

Note: If you signed up using Google SSO, there is no password to reset — you must reset your password through your Google account instead. TaskFlow cannot reset passwords for Google SSO accounts.

## Deleting Your Account
Account deletion is permanent and removes all your personal profile data. It does NOT delete a Workspace you own — Workspace deletion is a separate action and requires transferring ownership first (see Team Management document). To delete your personal account, go to **Profile Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This action cannot be undone.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
TaskFlow supports optional 2FA via authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.). To enable:
1. Go to **Profile Settings → Security**.
2. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication."
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

If you lose access to your 2FA device, contact support for identity verification and account recovery — this cannot be self-served for security reasons.

## Mobile App
TaskFlow has mobile apps for iOS and Android, available on the App Store and Google Play. The mobile app supports viewing and editing tasks, commenting, and notifications, but does not currently support Workspace administration (creating/deleting members, billing changes) — these must be done on desktop.
